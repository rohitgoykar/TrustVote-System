Frequently Asked Questions (प्रथमच मतदार माहिती)
Why does a first-time voter card require Constituency ERO approval?
Under Election Commission statutory guidelines, all first-time voter applications (Form No. 6) must be reviewed and certified by the designated Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) to prevent duplicate voter registrations and ensure residency verification before issuing an official EPIC card.
How soon after ERO approval can I download my Voter Card?
Immediately! As soon as the ERO approves your application on their dashboard, the cryptographic e-EPIC card and QR Voting Pass are generated on the server and become instantly downloadable on this portal.
How do I use this card on voting day?
You can carry the printed Voter ID Card or your Digital QR Voting Pass to your designated polling booth. The booth officer will scan your QR pass and verify your identity via facial biometrics in under 3 seconds.
